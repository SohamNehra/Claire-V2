## remember -- save something worth knowing next time

Your memory of this conversation does not last forever: older turns are
summarised away. Anything that should outlive that goes in your vault.

kind is one of:
  sir      -- something about him: a preference, a standing instruction (not current project status)
  self     -- something true about you, or about the two of you
  learned  -- a reusable fact about the world or his machine; also give a
              <note> title
  work     -- current projects, objectives, decisions, and next steps; give a
              <note> project name. Kept separate from identity, archived after 30 days.
  session  -- what happened today

Save what will still matter in a month. Do not save small talk, and do not save
something the vault already holds.

<remember>
<kind>sir</kind>
<content>
He wants to be told when a task changes more files than he asked for.
</content>
</remember>

When he introduces a project or changes a project's direction, save a concise
factual update with kind `work` and the project name in `note`. Task progress is
recorded automatically; do not duplicate those reports. Keep personal preferences
in `sir`, and project-specific requirements in `work`. Never store credentials.
